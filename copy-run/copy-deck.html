Absence findings

  • No review text was supplied, so no customer's own words could be mined. The strongest fact available is the preparation routine, and the copy leads with it instead of a borrowed adjective.
  • No prices, guarantees, accreditations or response times exist. Every line had to earn attention on service, place and preparation alone.

Cut

  • 'Tired Rooms? Painting and Decorating by TrueLine Finishes' (home H1): a question headline, and the operator ruled questions out.
  • 'Which Kind of Decorating Does Your Job Need?' (/services/ H1): named by the operator as the exact line to replace.
  • 'What Is It Like to Have Brandon In? Read the Reviews': owner-centric and a question; both banned in round 5.
  • 'Every card below is work Brandon carries out himself': the self-work trope the operator ordered removed outright.
  • 'The person you call is the person who does the work': same trope without the name, so it goes too.
  • 'Who Will Be Painting Your Home? Meet TrueLine Finishes': owner-centric framing in question form.
  • 'Your Local Decorating Experts' and 'Quality you can rely on, every time': unfalsifiable, unvisualisable, signable by anyone.
  • Any line naming 'Brandon Dawson' in marketing copy or metadata: the operator restricted the full name to legal identification.

Caveats

  • Every specific in this copy traces to a fact you provided or a review you supplied: check them before you spend money behind them. This writes the copy; it doesn't verify your claims or clear them for compliance.
  • No review text was available to mine: the site may not write reviews, ratings or counts, and the real reviews live inside Google-fed widgets. Harry Dry's best fact source was therefore closed, and the copy leans on the founder's preparation routine instead.
  • The keyword-plus-location H1s score 2 on rule three (a rival could sign them). That is a deliberate trade the operator instructed in round 5: the H1 answers the search, and the only-us burden moves to the subheads, the band line and the photographs.
  • 'Over eight years' experience', 'fully insured' and 'free quotations' are the client's own statements, used verbatim in the form the operator ruled. No cover amount, no price, no guarantee, no accreditation and no review number appears, because none exists.
