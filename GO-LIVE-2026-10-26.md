## After the switch

1. Wait for the zone to show **Active** (the dashboard can re-check now). The certificates then issue, usually within minutes.
2. `node scripts/verify-dns.mjs`, then `node scripts/verify-golive.mjs` (needs `dist/` built from the deployed commit). If any file differs, purge the zone cache and re-run (GO-LIVE law step 5).
3. **Onboard the domain for Email Sending** (dashboard only, no API): Compute & AI > Email Service > Email Sending > Onboard Domain > buildingsforlifeltd.com. Decline Email Routing if offered. Until then, leads are captured in D1 but no lead email can send from `leads@buildingsforlifeltd.com`.
