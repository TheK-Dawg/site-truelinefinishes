## Design and tokens
- **Template = Electrix (capture law).** Section order, square corners, the navy slab + yellow accent system, uppercase letter-spaced eyebrows, the boxed inner-page hero, the 3-blocks "How it works", the dark testimonial slab, the statement/stats slab and the four-column footer all come from `inputs/capture-electrix/`. Pricing and Team pages skipped (operator correction 5). The capture's service-details page is a 404 template; its 404 layout is reused for `/404`.
- **Token system = Astryx pattern, site equivalent.** `src/styles/tokens.css` is the only file with raw colours. Astryx warm neutrals kept verbatim. The Astryx orange brand is replaced by the capture's two-colour system mapped to BFL: Electrix navy -> deep builder green (the logo colour), Electrix yellow -> sandstone yellow. The Astryx type scale (11-30px) is kept and extended upward (36/44/56px) because a marketing hero cannot be set at 30px; the space scale is extended (64-120px) for section rhythm; breakpoints are an explicit scale (400-1180). `scripts/token-gate.mjs` enforces all of it (0 violations).
- **One font: system-ui stack.** Electrix uses Inter, which is banned. No webfont anywhere.
- **Headings at weight 500**, matching the capture (Electrix sets display headings medium, not bold).
